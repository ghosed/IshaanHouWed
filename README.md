This is a mobile-first, interactive digital dashboard and run-of-show web app for Ishaan & Pavani's Houston wedding weekend, complete with day/category/person filters, live completion tracking, WhatsApp status export, a ceremony rituals cue sheet, and a quick-dial vendor & escalation drawer.

What's Included

1) A Personal "My Tasks" & Multi-Filter Bar: Team members can select their own name from the dropdown (e.g., Dhruba, Partha, Arpita, Samik, Leya, Moumita, Maricella, Mita Shome, Buddhadev da, Trisha, etc.), filter by Day or Category (Ceremony Rituals, Logistics & Setup, Vendors Only), search by keyword, and toggle Hide Done.

2) Live Progress & WhatsApp Status Export: Checkmarks automatically save to the user's browser (localStorage), updating the top progress bar (0 / 47 Tasks Complete). Tapping Share copies a formatted summary of completed and pending tasks to the clipboard ready to paste into your family/coordinator WhatsApp group.

3) Ceremony Rituals Cue Sheet: Automatically highlights the 9-step Mandap sequence (Bor Agaman through Ashirbaad & Dhrubo Darshan) whenever Fri Wedding or Ceremony Rituals is selected.

4) Escalation & One-Tap Quick-Dial Drawer: Accessible from the sticky top header with pinned escalation leads (Partha & Arpita, Tapan & Dhruba), the Durgabari Thursday 10:00 AM access code note, and one-tap call buttons for all 13 vendors.
